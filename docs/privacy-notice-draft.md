## 2. What we collect, and why

We only collect what a specific feature you use needs. In plain terms:

| Data | Why we need it | Feature it powers |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone number | To identify your account and contact you | Login, appointments |
| Health information you enter (vitals, symptoms, meal logs) | To show you your own health trends and share them with a provider you choose to consult | Health Data Monitoring, Daily Meal Guidance |
| Lab results you upload or that a partner lab sends us | To store your reports in one place and let a doctor you consult see them | Laboratory Test Support |
| Wearable/device data (if you connect one) | To display your synced metrics alongside your manually logged data | Health Device integration |
| Call/appointment metadata (not call recordings, unless separately disclosed) | To schedule and remind you about consultations | Doctor Calls, Daily Monitoring Calls |
| Payment reference (not full card/UPI details — handled by our payment processor) | To confirm a subscription or paid consultation | Payments |

We do **not** sell your health data or use it for advertising. [CONFIRM AND KEEP TRUE.]

## 3. Your consent

- We ask for your **specific, informed, freely-given consent** before collecting anything beyond
  what's strictly needed to create your account.
- Consent for anything not essential to your care (e.g. marketing messages) is never a condition
  of using the core Platform.
- You can **withdraw consent** at any time from Settings → Privacy, as easily as you gave it. If
  you withdraw consent needed for a feature, we'll tell you which features stop working.

## 4. Your rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** your data, subject to records we're legally required to keep (e.g. a consultation
  record your doctor is obligated to retain under Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020).
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you're incapacitated or
  deceased.
- **File a grievance** with our Grievance Officer (§1), and afterward with the Data Protection
  Board of India if unresolved.

To exercise any of these, go to Settings → Privacy → My Data, or email [PRIVACY EMAIL].
We will respond within [X] days. [LAWYER: CONFIRM RESPONSE-TIME COMMITMENT.]
